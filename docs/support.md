# How You Can Support BojuBot

My name is Scott Kirvan, and I build BojuBot. It's a one-person project.

BojuBot is free and open source. If it's making your life easier and saving you some time, please consider supporting it. Everything helps cover my monthly server and devtool costs, so I can keep BojuBot running and create more free software.

## Ways to give

- **[Buy me a coffee on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/ScottKirvan)**: a one-off or monthly tip.
- **[Sponsor me on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/ScottKirvan)**: monthly or one-time sponsorship through GitHub.
- **Ask your company to sponsor.** If you use BojuBot at work, your employer can sponsor through either link above.

## Free ways to help

Money isn't the only thing that helps:

- **Star the repo** on [GitHub](https://github.com/ScottKirvan/BojuBot). It helps other people find BojuBot.
- **Report bugs.** Open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/ScottKirvan/BojuBot/issues) when something goes wrong. A clear report is worth a lot.
- **Suggest features.** Ideas are welcome as [GitHub issues](https://github.com/ScottKirvan/BojuBot/issues) too.
- **Tell others.** Mention BojuBot to friends, colleagues, or anyone who uses Obsidian.
- **Help out in Discord.** Answer questions and share what you've built on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/TN6XJSNK5Y).

## Thank you

To everyone who sponsors BojuBot: thank you. Your help makes a real and direct difference, and it's the reason I can keep working on it.
